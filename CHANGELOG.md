# Changelog

Changes below are based on the local version files, available scripts, and the Obsidian project record. V7 and later MP4 files are not included in this public-source project.

## V11
- Based on V10; added one small, tail-free, slowly drifting point star to the right of the moon, moving down-left and fading near the loop boundary.
- Preserved V10 meteor tracks and all other composited content.

## V10
- Based on V9; made both meteor paths trend from upper-right to lower-left while retaining their configured timing and appearance.

## V9
- Based on V8; repaired detected old streaks and rendered two separate meteor events, at 30 FPS.

## V8
- The available refined script applies a low-amplitude, local brightness/reflection field within an inset lake mask. It does not independently animate the cat tail or lantern.
- An earlier experimental script with a broader water mask and additional glint/lantern calculations is retained under `scripts/historical/`; it is not the recommended rebuild path.

## V7
- User-supplied/generated source MP4. The layered artwork source and exact original generation script were not found.
